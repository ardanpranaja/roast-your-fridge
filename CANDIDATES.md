# Candidate lines

Alternate roasts I considered before picking the entry line.

1. "My fridge is so old the ice maker retired, the light flickers, and it still thinks a jar of pickles counts as dinner." — Funny image, but the punchline wanders into a second idea instead of landing one twist.
2. "My fridge runs louder than a vacuum, colder than my ex, and yet somehow the milk still goes bad on day two." — Solid specifics, but "colder than my ex" is a worn-out joke everyone has heard.
3. "My fridge has a light that only switches on when I open the door, like it's embarrassed to be caught full of nothing but condiments." — Charming and specific, but too gentle; it observes instead of roasting.
4. "My fridge is the only roommate who eats my food, never pays rent, and hums show tunes at 3 a.m. to celebrate." — Good roommate twist, but "hums show tunes" is harder to believe than the lawnmower hum.
5. "My fridge beeps when I leave the door open, as if I forgot — buddy, you're the one who can't remember how to stay cold." — Nice comeback energy, but the twist needs the reader to know the door alarm first.

The chosen line won because it stacks three concrete, recognisable fridge flaws (hum, drip, warm milk) and pays them off with a single sharp twist — "one job, and it quit" — that reads cleanly aloud in one breath.
